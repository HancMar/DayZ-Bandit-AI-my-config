My config for "Bandit AI" mod in DayZ
It adds a bunch of waypoints and loadouts for bandit NPCs
Most of major towns and military bases have bandits/guards patrolling them so beware
There also are several tiers of bandits (those on the coast are weaker ofc)
GL have fun

PS.
Accuracy of bots got nerfed so if you want to increase it just change "accuracy" stat to ~75-80
